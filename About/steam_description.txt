[h1]Regions and Societies — Realistic Planets 2 Edition[/h1]
[b][url=https://github.com/Regions-and-societies/Core-MMF/wiki]Official Wiki and Documentation[/url][/b]
[b]Overhauls the world map with dynamic geographic region generation, world map overlays, and territory borders — built for Realistic Planets 2.[/b]

This is the Realistic Planets 2 build of Regions and Societies: identical to the standard mod except that it runs on the map-mode framework bundled with Realistic Planets 2. It divides the globe into geographic regions with defined borders, territorial ownership, a full demographic profile per region, and a living population that grows over time.

---

[h2]Best on a New Colony - Existing Saves Supported in Compatibility Mode[/h2]
Most of the work happens at world generation: region borders are drawn and faction settlements placed by them. Added to a world in progress, the mod adopts it in [b]Compatibility Mode[/b] - regions drawn, territory owned and shown, placement left to vanilla or whichever mod owns it. [b]A new colony is the recommended way to play.[/b] Removing the mod from an in-progress save is not supported.

---

[h2]Key Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Geographic Domain Mapping:[/b] regions drawn inside the terrain's natural sections, then cut into even pieces; every region is one connected landmass.
[*] [b]Territorial Ownership:[/b] a four-tier ladder from exclusive hold to loose claim, shaded on the map, with coastlines and mountains reading as real borders.
[*] [b]Geography-Driven Faction Placement:[/b] nations settle as contiguous territory - one settlement per region, intrusions cost goodwill, domains square off rather than spider.
[*] [b]Regional Demographics:[/b] a deterministic profile per region across seven axes - age, sex ratio, xenotypes, ideoligions, wealth, education, employment - from the factions pressing on it, their tech and beliefs, and the land. Nothing is stored in your save.
[*] [b]Region Inspection Panel:[/b] Region (shape mini-map, ownership, features, resources, wildlife), Population (the seven axes as bars and pies) and Economy tabs.
[*] [b]Overlays Under One Menu:[/b] territory, borders, population density, natural barriers, residences and the seven demographic overlays under a single button, running on Realistic Planets 2's bundled map-mode framework.
[*] [b]Hooks for Other Mods:[/b] war and drafting mods can skew a region's sex ratio; expansion mods can rank territory with core's compactness metric.
[/list]

[h2]Changelog (v0.3.0) - Living Population Model[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]New region partitioning[/b] inside the terrain's natural sections, with a pluggable algorithm (contain-then-subdivide, or the legacy anchor-Voronoi look) stamped on each world so old saves are never re-cut.
[*] [b]Region inspection panel[/b] with Region, Population and Economy tabs.
[*] [b]Faction character:[/b] raiders read as raiders, an imperial court as an elite; education is a five-level schooling ladder with real meaning.
[*] [b]Settlement growth[/b] from in-game birthrates, a [b]residence layer[/b], and all overlays consolidated under one menu button.
[*] [b]Population scale:[/b] populations three times larger, worlds start settled, and the terrain-aware sprawl is the one population field - heatmap, labels and region totals agree. Heatmap runs violet to yellow.
[*] [b]Performance:[/b] demographics precomputed at load. Worldgen measured with all five DLCs and all four patches: 24 s at 30% coverage, about 3 minutes at 100%. Settlement road-linking and worldgen outpost seeding are out of this release and return reworked in 0.4.0.
[*] [b]Odyssey[/b] orbit-layer objects handled cleanly; [b]Layered Atmosphere and Orbit[/b] now compatible.
[*] [b]Fixed (thanks to 96localk):[/b] every generated faction was created hidden with no leader. Factions now generate visible with leaders, only chosen factions appear, and a one-time repair fixes old saves.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility Matrix[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]All five DLCs:[/b] compatible; Odyssey space factions generate on the orbit layer, terrestrial factions on the surface.
[*] [b]Realistic Planets 2:[/b] required - it supplies the map-mode framework this edition runs on.
[*] [b]Map Mode Framework (standalone) and the standard edition:[/b] incompatible with this edition by design - only one map-mode framework may load.
[*] [b]Layered Atmosphere and Orbit:[/b] compatible since 0.3.0.
[*] [b]Empire, World Domination, Vanilla Factions Expanded, Vanilla Outposts Expanded:[/b] supported through the companion patches above.
[*] [b]Existing saves:[/b] Compatibility Mode - regions and territory drawn, placement rules stand down.
[/list]
