[h1]Regions and Societies — Realistic Planets 2 Edition[/h1]
[b][url=https://github.com/Regions-and-societies/Core-MMF/wiki]Official Wiki and Documentation[/url][/b]
[b]Overhauls the world map with dynamic geographic region generation, world map overlays, and territory borders — built for Realistic Planets 2.[/b]

This is the Realistic Planets 2 build of Regions and Societies: identical to the standard mod except that it runs on the map-mode framework bundled with Realistic Planets 2. It divides the globe into geographic regions with defined borders, territorial ownership, a full demographic profile per region, and a living population that grows over time.

---

[h2]Best on a New Colony - Existing Saves Supported in Compatibility Mode[/h2]
Most of the work happens at world generation: region borders are drawn and faction settlements placed by them. Added to a world in progress, the mod adopts it in [b]Compatibility Mode[/b] - regions drawn, territory owned and shown, placement left to vanilla or whichever mod owns it. [b]A new colony is the recommended way to play.[/b] Removing the mod from an in-progress save is not supported.

---

[h2]Key Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Geographic Domain Mapping:[/b] regions drawn inside the terrain's natural sections and cut into even, rounded cells; inland lakes shared between their shores, small islands folded into the mainland, every region one connected landmass.
[*] [b]Territorial Ownership:[/b] a four-tier ladder from exclusive hold to loose claim, shaded on the map; a faction holding half a region earns its coasts, rock and ridges as a secure border.
[*] [b]Geography-Driven Faction Placement:[/b] factions settle by a biome's real liveability, cluster or scatter to taste, and scattered low-tech peoples split into regional kin factions - all set up per faction in the new-game Geographic Placement dialog.
[*] [b]Regional Demographics:[/b] a deterministic profile per region across seven axes - age, sex ratio, xenotypes, ideoligions, wealth, education, employment - from the factions pressing on it, their tech and beliefs, and the land. Nothing is stored in your save.
[*] [b]Region Inspection Panel:[/b] Region (shape mini-map, ownership, features, resources, wildlife), Population (the seven axes as bars and pies) and Economy tabs.
[*] [b]Overlays Under One Menu:[/b] territory, borders, population density, natural barriers, residences and the seven demographic overlays under a single button, running on Realistic Planets 2's bundled map-mode framework.
[*] [b]Hooks for Other Mods:[/b] war and drafting mods can skew a region's sex ratio; expansion mods can rank territory with core's compactness metric.
[/list]

[h2]Changelog (v0.4.0) - Map & Placement[/h2]
The world map is redrawn to follow the land, and factions settle it with character. A new-game [b]Geographic Placement[/b] dialog puts territory shares, clustering and kin in your hands.
[b]The map follows the land[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Inland lakes[/b] are shared between their shores instead of ringing themselves with a border; [b]small islands[/b] join the mainland, and island chains (30+ tiles) form archipelago regions.
[*] [b]Tiny slivers[/b] are folded into a neighbour or dropped when isolated (or kept, with an option); [b]mountain passes and land bridges[/b] read as borders instead of letting a region span two places.
[*] [b]Rounder regions[/b] (relaxed-honeycomb default; 0.3.0 and 0.2.x still selectable) and one [b]Target region size[/b] knob with a per-biome editor.
[/list]
[b]Factions settle with character[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Habitability-driven placement[/b] weighs a biome's real liveability and avoids crowding, so raiders stop homesteading on the ice.
[*] [b]Per-faction clustering[/b] (sprawl, a set number of footholds, or scatter) and [b]regional kin sub-factions[/b] that split a scattered low-tech people into compass-named kin.
[*] [b]Ownership earns its walls:[/b] a faction holding half a region counts its coasts, rock and ridges as a secure border.
[/list]
[b]Setup, control and performance[/b]
[list]
[*] The [b]Geographic Placement[/b] dialog sets each faction's share of the world before you generate it, with a live region-count estimate, a share pie and hostility dots.
[*] A [b]Societies master toggle[/b] disables the whole population/demographics/economy layer; a [b]World maturity[/b] slider and mid-game [b]region lock[/b] govern seeded holdings and rival-territory settling.
[*] Worlds reproduce from a seed (stamped [b]Worldgen: v0.4.0[/b]); existing saves keep their map. [b]World generation's partition step is ~28x faster[/b] - a measured world dropped from ~82 seconds to under 3, with an identical map.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility Matrix[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]All five DLCs:[/b] compatible; Odyssey space factions generate on the orbit layer, terrestrial factions on the surface.
[*] [b]Realistic Planets 2:[/b] required - it supplies the map-mode framework this edition runs on.
[*] [b]Map Mode Framework (standalone) and the standard edition:[/b] incompatible with this edition by design - only one map-mode framework may load.
[*] [b]Layered Atmosphere and Orbit:[/b] compatible since 0.3.0.
[*] [b]Empire, World Domination, Vanilla Factions Expanded, Vanilla Outposts Expanded:[/b] supported through the companion patches above.
[*] [b]Existing saves:[/b] Compatibility Mode - regions and territory drawn, placement rules stand down.
[/list]
